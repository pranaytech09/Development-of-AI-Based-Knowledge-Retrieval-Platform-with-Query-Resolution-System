# AI and Machine Learning Guide

## What is AI?
Artificial Intelligence refers to computer systems designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.

## Machine Learning
Machine Learning is a subset of AI that focuses on enabling systems to learn and improve from experience.

## Deep Learning
Deep Learning uses neural networks with multiple layers to process complex patterns.

## Applications
- Image Recognition
- Natural Language Processing
- Predictive Analytics
- Robotics
- Autonomous Vehicles

## Conclusion
AI is transforming technology and business.
